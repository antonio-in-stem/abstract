Example

Release policy.

A deliberately small example showing that Abstract can also model internal policy records, not just catalog data.

schema Policy {
    id: text(1..40)
    level: enum(info, warning, critical)
    applies_to[]: enum(product, release, dataset)
    rule: text(1..180)
}
Policy :: @id.public_release_checks, @level.critical
    applies_to: [product, release]
    rule: "Public releases must declare an owner, a stable status, and at least one documented capability."

Small schemas are often the most useful schemas.
